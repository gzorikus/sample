# Take feedback

**Open when** the owner names a defect, a doubt or a wish — before any  
fix.

**Take his word as a case, not a mood.** Take three things: the case —  
a quote, a file:line, a command; the boundary — when it fires and when  
not; the expected result. No case: ask for it, one question.

**Treat a paraphrase as not his word.** Quote by place or ask again  
(rule 33).

1. **Record the pitfall or the bug before the fix**, in the `fixup!`  
   that carries it: the case, the boundary, the expected. **A fix  
   whose body does not carry the record is the defect.**
2. **Reproduce.** Run a command whose output shows the defect, or  
   quote it at its place.
3. **Find the home.** The law, a prompt, the convention (the project's  
   answer), the questionnaire (a project's fact), the map (the  
   harness). One home per fact; restate nothing.
4. **Correct or add the instruction where it is read** — strictly  
   imperative: what to do, the test, the boundary, the defect (rule  
   38). No reasons, no options, no «you».
5. **Mechanize where it is checkable**: the entry, its acceptance, its  
   mechanism. Not checkable: `manual`, and say what a person reads.
6. **Land.** Carry the record and the corrected instruction in the  
   `fixup!`; keep the author date, pass the date test, push.
7. **Verify.** Run every acceptance the entry names; the map's lint;  
   the text's check; the links; a second run changes nothing. Report  
   the commands and their output.

**Treat a check not run as a claim.** Say "not verified", and what  
would verify it.
